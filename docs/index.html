      Decision Twins

      Two similar businesses, different outcomes

      Pairs are matched on designated core features, not claimed to be economically identical. The capital-injection case is a conceptual example unless numbers come from the engine.

      Business A

      Presents normal operating cash flow.

      Likely treated as ordinary risk.

      Business B

      Receives a large manual capital injection shortly before assessment.

      May look safer if a temporary balance is treated as durable strength.

      A capital injection is not inherently illegitimate. The integrity risk is mistaking a non-recurring presentation effect for sustainable repayment capacity.
